ROYAL CODEX

Rules of Royal Ludo

1. Unlocking From Yard

A pawn can only leave its colored base when you roll a 6. It enters on your designated starting star square.

2. Extra Rolls

Rolling a 6 or capturing an opponent's pawn grants an immediate bonus roll. However, rolling three consecutive 6s voids the third turn!

3. Capturing Opponents

Landing on a square occupied by an opponent sends their pawn back to their yard. Captures are rewarded with a bonus roll.

4. Consecrated Safe Squares (★)

The 8 star squares (starting squares + mid-arm stars) are completely safe. Multiple pawns of any color can co-exist here peacefully without fear of capture.

5. Entering the Home Triangle

Once completing the 52-tile circuit, your pawns traverse the colored Home Lane. Reaching the central victory triangle requires an exact roll.
